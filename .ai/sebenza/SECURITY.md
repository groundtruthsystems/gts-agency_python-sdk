| T-005 | Elevation of privilege | A malformed row in the annotations delegate's paginated reads could make a naive end-of-page test (`len(items) < size`) misread a full page as the last one and stop early — on the job-transition ledger this could skip a later row carrying `prior_actor_conflict`, letting a separation-of-duties violation on clinical rule approval go undetected | Annotation job/transition list reads (SDK) ↔ consumer separation-of-duties check | High | Medium | Mitigated | `TolerantPage.row_count` (`items` + `rejected`) is the documented end-of-page test, with the wrong (`len(items)`) and right (`row_count`) idioms shown side by side in `docs/annotations.md`; the false "unchanged for non-reading callers" docstring claim was corrected. | T-010 | Tampering | Opaque `Any`-typed job fields (`annotation_data`, `checklist_state`, `delta`, `vertex_data`, `connected_vertices`, `connected_edges`) pass through the SDK completely unvalidated; a malformed or unexpected shape from either front-end can silently corrupt a consumer's integrity check — observed by the consumer as dropped `part_of` edges publishing while its closure check reported zero problems | Annotation job payload fields (`Any`-typed) ↔ consumer contamination/closure check | Medium | Medium | Accepted | Deliberate: comand does whole-value column replacement and two front-ends write different shapes into the same columns, so a validating model would reject one of them and blind the consumer instead of protecting it. | T-006 | Elevation of privilege | comand silently clamps `list_job_transitions`' `size` to 500 (the endpoint's only clamp, and it reports nothing); a caller requesting more gets exactly 500 back, and a "fewer rows than asked for" end-of-page test reads the clamp as the last page, permanently missing any transition — including a `prior_actor_conflict` flag — beyond row 500 | Job-transition ledger (comand) ↔ SDK caller pagination | High | Low | Open | Documented only (docstring, `size` parameter note, `docs/annotations.md` failure-mode table) advising callers to keep `size` ≤ 500 or treat an exact-500 return as suspect rather than end-of-page. | T-002 | Elevation of privilege | A principal holding workflow-execute but not annotations-write can change which workflow governs an existing batch, since `bind_workflow` is authorized on a different permission from the rest of the annotations surface | Annotation batch ↔ workflow-execute authorization boundary (comand API) | Medium | Low | Open | Server-side only, and narrower than it first appears: `bind_workflow` additionally requires organisation membership (non-members get 404) and accepts only published versions of a workflow matching the batch type. Residual gap: within an org, workflow-execute alone suffices to re-govern a batch the holder could not otherwise administer | `annotation_workflow_binding_20260822` | 2026-08-22 |
| T-003 | Tampering | Re-binding a batch's workflow after some of its jobs are already accepted retroactively changes the review rigour applied to completed clinical rule work | Accepted annotation jobs ↔ workflow governance binding | Medium | Low | Mitigated | Server requires a non-empty written reason whenever the incoming version disables self-approval protection or the job type already has accepted work and jobs would be affected; it rejects a version missing any state the affected jobs occupy, re-checks batch-type compatibility on every rebind, and reports `jobs_regoverned` for audit. The SDK passes `rebind_reason` through rather than inventing one | `annotation_workflow_binding_20260822` | 2026-08-22 |
| T-004 | Information disclosure | Clinical rule graph payloads (up to 50 MiB) cross the agent-to-control-plane boundary without transport encryption enforced by the SDK, exposing guideline content to eavesdropping wherever TLS is not terminated | Agent process ↔ control-plane API (network transport) | Medium | Low | Accepted | TLS is a deployment property; the SDK never downgrades or disables transport security, and local development runs plain HTTP by choice. | T-009 | Information disclosure | A caller cannot distinguish a RESTRICTED annotation batch they are not an active member of from a batch that does not exist, since the server's confidentiality filter removes it from `list_batches` entirely rather than rejecting it — masking access-control or membership misconfiguration from anyone auditing coverage | Annotation batch list (confidentiality filter) ↔ caller audit visibility | Low | Low | Mitigated | Deliberate server-side filter (`annotation_repository.rs:413`: batch excluded unless `confidentiality_level` is unrestricted or the caller has an active membership); documented in `docs/annotations.md` so a caller checks `list_batch_members` before assuming non-existence | `annotation_job_reads_20260831` | 2026-09-17 |
